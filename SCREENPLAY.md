# The Rich Abuja Girl (Screenplay & Storyboard Metadata)

**Genre**: Contemporary Nigerian Romance / High-Society Drama  
**Setting**: Abuja, Nigeria (Maitama, Asokoro, Jabi Lake, Wuse II, Millennium Park)  

## Main Characters (Yoruba)
- **Tiwa Adenuga**: Elegant, compassionate 24-year-old Yoruba heiress & fashion designer. Daughter of Chief Adebayo Adenuga.
- **Tunde Bakare**: Brilliant 26-year-old solar technology engineer and innovator from humble roots.
- **Chief Adebayo Adenuga**: Tiwa's father, a powerful, stern Yoruba industrialist and high-society chief.
- **Bisi**: Tiwa's vibrant, energetic best friend and confidante.
- **Segun Lawson**: Arrogant heir to Lawson Holdings, favored suitor backed by Chief Adenuga.

  - Voiceover: "Tunde didn't care about my designer shoes or famous surname. He wiped grease off his hands, smiled, and fixed the engine in ten minutes."
